About

Designing for minds
that work differently

I am a design researcher, educator, and entrepreneur with a Ph.D. in Design from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati. My doctoral work focused on visual design guidelines for curriculum-based digital content tailored to children living with ADHD in typical learning environments — a body of research that sits at the intersection of cognitive psychology, educational technology, and visual communication.

As Assistant Professor (Grade II) at IIIT Dharwad, I design and deliver minor courses in design for B.Tech students, serve on the Internal Complaints Committee, and mentor startup teams incubated at IDRP. I also co-chair and review for prestigious HCI conferences including India HCI and ACM CHI.

My entrepreneurial work led me to found Hey Attrangi Pvt. Ltd., a digital healthcare startup providing solutions for neurodivergent children and supporting the mental health of college students. I also serve as Technical Consultant and Board Member at Aarogya Aadhar — a health-services startup focused on emergency medical support and low-cost patient care.

I received my research fellowship from the Ministry of Education, Government of India, and have been recognised with grants from ACM CHI and featured in Beyond Science Magazine.
