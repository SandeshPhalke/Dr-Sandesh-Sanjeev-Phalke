About Me

Designing for minds that work differently

Currently serving as Assistant Professor (Grade II, Level 11) at the Department of Art, Science & Design, IIIT Dharwad — designing and facilitating minor courses in design for B.Tech students across CSE and ECE programmes, serving on the Internal Complaints Committee, and mentoring startups incubated at IDRP.

Founder & Director of Aatrangi Pvt. Ltd. (heyattrangi.com) — a digital mental well-being startup providing technology-based solutions for mental wellbeing and further supports the neurodivergent children. Building equitable technology at the intersection of design research and healthcare.

Ph.D. in Design from IIT Guwahati. Doctoral research on visual design guidelines for curriculum-based digital content tailored to children with ADHD — at the intersection of cognitive psychology, educational technology, and visual communication. Nine years of field research across government schools in Assam, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.

Technical Consultant & Board Member at Aarogya Aadhar Pvt. Ltd., Pune — a health-services startup focused on emergency medical support and affordable patient care.

MoE Ph.D. Fellow (Govt. of India) · ACM Member since November 2023 · Co-General Chair, India HCI 2025 · Workshop PI for ANRF-funded Futures of Design workshop · Steering Committee Member, NAIN 2.0, Govt. of Karnataka · PMU Mentor, NAIN

Focus Areas

Active Research Areas

01
Visual Design for Children with ADHD
Developing and validating visual design guidelines for curriculum-based digital content representation for children with ADHD in typical Indian classroom environments. Longitudinal studies across government schools in Assam, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.
02
Child-Computer Interaction & HCI4D
Participatory co-design methods centred on children in under-resourced Indian government school contexts. Research on how digital tools can serve India's diverse learner population at scale.
03
Inclusive Education & EdTech
Designing technology-based learning interventions that work across ability spectrums and economic conditions. ANRF-funded projects connecting design research to grassroots educational impact.
04
Interdisciplinary Manufacturing & Design
Advanced manufacturing processes including laser machining of Ti6Al4V, laser cladding, and precision manufacturing — bridging engineering and design research traditions.
